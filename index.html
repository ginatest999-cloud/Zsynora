Featured Workflow Blueprint

A Connected Sales Journey for Automotive Businesses

From the first enquiry to customer follow-up, ZSynora helps automotive businesses connect their sales process and reduce manual coordination.

STAGE 01

Lead Capture

Inbound enquiries from digital ads, portal listings, or walk-ins are instantly logged with vehicle interest.

STAGE 02

Sales Rep Assignment

Automatic round-robin routing assigns the lead to the right dealership executive with instant WhatsApp alerts.

STAGE 03

Vehicle & Variant Matching

Customer preferences, color options, and pricing sheets are linked directly to the buyer's CRM profile.

STAGE 04

Test Drive Booking

Automated slot scheduling with SMS/WhatsApp confirmation and driver license verification tracking.

STAGE 05

Automated Follow-up

Timed follow-up notifications ensure no prospect drops off between test drive and quotation review.

STAGE 06

Deal & Financing

Quotation generation, loan processing status, insurance documentation, and approval workflows.

STAGE 07

Delivery Coordination

Checklist tracking for PDI (pre-delivery inspection), accessories fitting, registration, and handover.

STAGE 08

Customer Retention

First service reminders, insurance renewal notices, and customer satisfaction feedback surveys.
