Detailed Industry Journey

A Connected Sales Journey for Automotive Dealerships

Automotive vehicle sales involve multiple touchpoints across enquiries, showroom visits, test drives, finance approvals, and delivery. Here is how ZSynora designs an integrated workflow.

Phase 1: Inbound to Engagement

1. Enquiry & Intelligent Routing

  • ✓Digital Ad & Walk-in Capture: Leads from Meta, Google Ads, portal listings, and showroom reception enter CRM immediately.
  • ✓Executive Round-Robin: The system assigns the prospect to an active sales consultant based on showroom shifts or model expertise.
  • ✓Instant WhatsApp Welcome: The buyer receives a personalized digital brochure and sales consultant contact details instantly.
Phase 2: Experience & Evaluation

2. Test Drive & Variant Alignment

  • ✓Slot & Vehicle Allocation: Automated calendar booking prevents vehicle double-booking for demo models.
  • ✓License & Feedback Form: Digital pre-drive consent and post-drive feedback capture directly on the consultant's phone.
  • ✓Quotation Generation: Dynamic on-road price calculation including variant options, accessories, and insurance packages.
Phase 3: Deal Closure & Finance

3. Booking & Loan Approvals

  • ✓Booking Advance Tracking: Integration with payment gateway logs booking amount and locks vehicle allotment.
  • ✓Finance & KYC Workflow: Bank loan document checklist and approval milestone tracking between finance desk and buyer.
  • ✓Management Approval: Any special discount requires one-tap mobile approval from the Sales Head.
Phase 4: Delivery & Retention

4. PDI, Delivery & Aftersales

  • ✓PDI & Fitment Checklist: Workshop checklist verifies accessories fitting, registration number, and cleaning before handover.
  • ✓Digital Delivery Docket: Customer signs digital delivery confirmation with photo record stored in CRM.
  • ✓Automated Service Reminders: First free service, periodic maintenance, and annual insurance renewal triggers.
