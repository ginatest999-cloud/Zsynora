Our Consulting & Implementation Services

Practical Systems Tailored to Your Business

From initial CRM architecture to automated multi-system integrations, we build reliable digital workflows that eliminate operational bottlenecks and drive scalable growth.

Service 01

Zoho CRM Implementation

Set up a CRM that matches the way your business actually works—from first lead enquiry to repeat customer management.

A generic CRM with default fields creates clutter and low team adoption. We configure structured modules, tailored pipeline stages, automated data capture, and customized permissions so your sales team stays organized and accountable.

What We Configure & Deliver:

  • ✓Complete CRM Account Setup & Organization Hierarchy
  • ✓Leads, Contacts, Accounts & Deals Module Alignment
  • ✓Multi-stage Custom Sales Pipelines & Deal Probability
  • ✓Custom Fields, Layout Rules & Dynamic Conditional Sections
  • ✓Roles, Profiles & Data Security Permissions
  • ✓Executive Dashboards & Automated Scheduled Reports

Automated Workflows & Rules:

  • ✓Multi-level Approval Workflows (Discounts, Quotes, Leave)
  • ✓Instant Notification Triggers (Email, SMS & WhatsApp)
  • ✓Automatic Task Generation & Follow-up Schedules
  • ✓Lead Assignment Rules (Round-Robin, Geography, Value)
  • ✓Scheduled Background Jobs & Data Maintenance Checks
  • ✓Automated Invoicing & Payment Status Updates
Service 02

Business Process Automation

Replace repetitive manual work with reliable automated workflows that execute flawlessly without human intervention.

Don't rely on staff remembering to follow up with a lead after 3 days or manually notifying a manager for discount approvals. We translate your standard operating procedures into dependable automated rules.

Configure business applications around your unique requirements instead of forcing your business into a rigid generic box.

Every industry has unique data points, custom calculation logic, and validation criteria. We build bespoke modules, automated functions, and tailored layout rules that reflect your exact operations.

  • ✓Tailored Report Formats & KPI Visualization Widgets

Migration Protocol & Quality Checks:

  • ✓Pre-import Sandbox Validation & Zero-loss Guarantee
Service 04

Data Migration

Move your existing customer records, active deals, and historical data into a structured system without starting from scratch.

Migrating from chaotic spreadsheets or outdated software can feel overwhelming. We handle the data cleanup, field mapping, deduplication, and structured import so your business transitions smoothly with zero data loss.

Connect the applications your team already uses so information moves automatically between systems in real time.

Connected Systems:
